home page of alysa zhao

I am a combined bachelor's and master's student at Boston University studying pure math. My current aspiration is to be able to understand homotopy type theory. This means I am working on understanding higher categories and formalization. I am deeply motivated by curiosities in the foundations of mathematics.

I held the co-president position as an undergraduate at the Mathematics Association at BU, where we work jointly with Society of Mathematics to facilitate student-led lectures, competition preparation sessions, and academic counseloring opportunities.

I am also involved in Logic @ BU.

Outside of math, I am an avid reader, especially of critical literature and philosophies of language, and am a senior editor at Arche. I am particularly interested in ideas about reference, interpretation, and difference. I am also passionate about music, and sometimes even make some.
